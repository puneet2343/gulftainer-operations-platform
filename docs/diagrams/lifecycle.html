Import container lifecycle and release conditions

Physical state, commercial clearance and custody are tracked separately. A container is released only when every clearance condition is met; every step writes an immutable event and an audit entry.

Physical
ExpectedOn the discharge list (BAPLIE) of an inbound vessel call
DischargedYard position assigned; stevedoring charged to the line; storage clock starts
In yardShifts, scanning, reefer checks; holds block release
Inland transferUncollected after 3 days: moved to SICD or Sajaa for the cargo interests' account
Gated outE-token checked at the marshalling yard; EIR with seal and condition at the gate
DeliveredTransport job closed with proof of delivery, or collected by the haulier
Clearance
CustomsClearance message or manual declaration; inspection places a customs hold
Delivery orderThe line's release authority, with a validity date
Charges settledOnline card payment of storage and service lines: full, partial or zero
Haulier assignedConsignee assigns on the customer portal
E-tokenTwo-hour slot, AED 25 import pick-up fee; cannot be deleted after marshalling yard entry
ReleasedThe gate re-checks every condition before gate-out
Custody
CarrierOn the vessel
Terminal operatorFrom discharge to gate-out, including depot storage
HaulierFrom gate-out, recorded on the EIR
ConsigneeFrom proof of delivery

Rules enforced by the services

Every write is one transaction; a failed validation writes nothing
Gate, discharge, load, receiving and picking are idempotent by key
Holds cancel active e-tokens; customs holds lift only through the customs message
Export drop-offs are accepted only inside the vessel's gate window and need a VGM
Storage: 10 free days (1 for DG), then day slabs from the tariff
Stock adjustments above the threshold need a second approver, never the requester
